## CISSP (part 1 of 2)

CISSP is an Advanced / Senior Cybersecurity certification from (ISC)².
- Official link: https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp
- Exam cost: ~$749
- Prerequisites: 5 years paid full-time work in 2+ of 8 CISSP domains; 4-year degree waives 1 year; Students CAN take exam → earn 'Associate of (ISC)²' → 6 years to gain experience; recommended: 5 years (or 4 with degree waiver)
- Exam format: 125–175 questions (CAT format); 4 hours; valid for 3 years (40 CPE credits/year)
- Best for: Security Manager, CISO, Senior Security Analyst
- Student discounts / free resources: None specific
