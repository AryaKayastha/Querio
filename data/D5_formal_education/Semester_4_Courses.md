## Semester 4 subjects

Subjects (courses) in the Semester 4 syllabus -- B.Tech CE/CSE/IT, Second Year, academic year 2025-26. Total 28 credits:

- ITUC202 Fundamentals of Database Management Systems (5 credits)
- CEUC201 Fundamentals of Software Engineering (4 credits)
- CEUC202 Computer Organization and Architecture (3 credits)
- ITUE204 Design and Analysis of Algorithms (5 credits)
- Elective - I (5 credits) -- choose one from the Semester 4 Elective - I list
- ITUP201 / CEUP201 / CSUP201 Software Group Project (2 credits)
- University Elective - II (2 credits) -- choose one from the Semester 4 University Elective - II list
- HSUV202 Human Values and Ethics OR HSUV206 Life Lessons from Ramayan and Mahabharat (2 credits)
